## Per-screen audit questions (the ten questions from the brief), for the screens that matter

### Swap (`before/32-swap-1nav-available-0.png`)
1. **Goal:** exchange NAV for another asset. 2. **Primary action:** Review. 3. **Obvious?** Yes, but disabled with a misleading reason. 4. **User needs to know:** how much they can swap and what it costs. 5. **Forced to know unnecessarily:** that NAV "on the exchange" and NAV "in the wallet" are different balances; that a bridge exists; the 10 USDC / 1 NAV minimums as a combined sentence. 6. **Remove:** "Available: 0" as the headline number; the wallet hint paragraph. 7. **Automate:** everything the router already automates — but *say so up front*. 8. **Belongs elsewhere:** nothing. 9. **Hidden prerequisites:** account creation (faucet, 28.9 NAV), registration, bridge deposit + 3 confirmations. 10. **Interrupted:** operation persists in localStorage and resumes, but is only visible on three pages.

### Bridge deposit (`before/13-…`, `before/15-…`)
1. Show where to send NAV from *another* wallet. 2. Activate. 3. Obvious, but wrong order (Activate fails until the faucet ran). 4. "You need a one-time setup costing ~29 NAV". 5. HyperCore / HYPE / gas / register. 6. Both buttons. 7. Both actions (they are deterministic). 8. The setup belongs inside the Swap/Deposit intent; the address belongs under NAV → Receive from another wallet. 9. Account existence, gas, registration. 10. Faucet payment sent → user closes modal → page re-checks only on re-open; nothing tracks the 3-confirmation wait.

### Operation card (`before/39-…`)
1. Show progress. 2. Try again / It went through. 3. Yes. 4. What is happening now and how long. 5. Nothing protocol-specific (good). 6. "It went through / Send again" — the app can check. 7. Interrupted-step verification. 8. A global tray + a full page, not only three embedded spots. 9. –. 10. Resume works (engine), visibility does not.
